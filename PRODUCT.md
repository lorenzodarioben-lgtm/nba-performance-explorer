# NBA Performance Explorer

## Platform

Web. Desktop, tablet and mobile browsers; keyboard navigation, touch and reduced motion.

## Product Purpose

Explore NBA players, compare seasons and players, and examine recorded games and historical
seasons with visible data sources and limitations. Research views retain source, cohort
and formula context. This purpose is documented in the existing README and application copy.

## Users

Primary audience segmentation has not been specified. Existing workflows support readers
exploring basketball records and people studying source-backed NBA data; no narrower
demographic or business positioning is assumed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve existing player profiles, comparisons, scoreboard, historical navigation,
  season-aware logos, analytics, charts, global search and browser-local saved research.
- Historical season and champion statements come from Wikidata. Preserve source entity IDs,
  labels, links and missing fields. Do not infer historical aliases, NBA team mappings or
  championship totals from that source slice.
- Separate licensed recorded tables retain their own provenance. Demo fixtures must be
  labelled. Do not invent NBA facts or present unavailable records as verified data.
- The owner requires usable desktop/mobile layouts, keyboard access, honest loading/error
  states, and static touch/reduced-motion fallbacks for decorative liquid motion.
- This local implementation task does not authorize merging, deployment or a pull request.
