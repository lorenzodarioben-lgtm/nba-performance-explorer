# Final feature matrix — NBA Performance Explorer 3.0

The authoritative closeout of the 134 requirements in the original Codex specification ([archived ledger](../expansion/FEATURE_LEDGER.md), [master spec](../expansion/SHOT_1_MASTER_SPEC.md)). The archived ledger is unchanged; this matrix records each requirement’s status after the Arena & Film Room redesign and the three NBA 3.0 expansion sessions, with the evidence for every change.

Classifications follow the original definitions. **VERIFIED**: the complete requested behaviour works within the requirement’s explicitly permitted scope, with tests. **PARTIAL**: useful behaviour exists, part is genuinely missing. **BLOCKED**: an essential data source or permitted access path is unavailable. **DEFERRED**: deliberately excluded. A route existing, or a component rendering fixtures, never counts as VERIFIED.

## What prevents 100%

- **No current or live league feed is integrated.** NBA.com Stats is used only for player research (it answered from the development host at the final check, though it timed out in earlier sessions); no permitted, verified scoreboard, schedule or standings feed exists in the app. Current scoreboards, upcoming games, current standings and live freshness (A02, A05, A06, A08, A09, D01, D02, E01) cannot be honestly completed with historical archives.
- **No play-by-play or possession data under a usable licence.** Play-by-play, scoring runs, lead changes, scrubbers, ratings, on/off and lineup ratings (C07, D06, D07, D08, D12, J04, J05) stay blocked; the film room deliberately describes quarter totals only.
- **One recorded season.** The 2022–23 team pages failed the strict audit (10 of 30 clean), so historical leaderboards, records, progression and era views (E04–E10, I02, I03, I05, I08, I09, I11) remain single-season or bounded.
- **No representative league shot baseline** (G09), **no era model** (F09, I10), **no injury data** (B15), **no dated franchise mapping** (I06, deferred).
- **Fields the sources do not publish:** shooting attempts for team aggregates (C06), positions in leader filters (E08, H05), player experience (C10), full box scores (D04, D09).
