# Final feature matrix — NBA Performance Explorer 3.2

The authoritative closeout of the 134 requirements in the original Codex specification ([archived ledger](../expansion/FEATURE_LEDGER.md), [master spec](../expansion/SHOT_1_MASTER_SPEC.md)). The archived ledger is unchanged; this matrix records each requirement’s status after the Arena & Film Room redesign, the three NBA 3.0 expansion sessions, the 3.1 completion sprint and the 3.2 completion project, with the evidence for every change. The 3.2 log and reports are in [`docs/expansion5`](../expansion5/ORIGINAL_SPEC_COMPLETION_REPORT.md); 3.1’s are in [`docs/expansion4`](../expansion4/GAP_CLOSURE_PLAN.md).

Classifications follow the original definitions. **VERIFIED**: the complete requested behaviour works within the requirement’s explicitly permitted scope, with tests. **PARTIAL**: useful behaviour exists, part is genuinely missing. **BLOCKED**: an essential data source or permitted access path is unavailable. **DEFERRED**: deliberately excluded. A route existing, or a component rendering fixtures, never counts as VERIFIED.

## Remaining limits after 3.2

Every requirement is VERIFIED within its stated scope. The scopes that remain, by dependency:

- **NBA.com availability.** Live scores, schedules, current standings, play-by-play, lineups and on/off are fetched on request and cached; offline they say they are unavailable and the committed archive keeps working. Their availability from a deployed host has not been checked.
- **What NBA.com publishes.** Play-by-play from 1996-97, possessions and ratings from 1996-97, lineups and on/off from 2007-08. Eleven seasons (1983-84 to 1986-87, 1988-89, 1990-91 to 1995-96) fail the box-score gate and are team-only.
- **Bounded datasets.** Shot locations cover the 2017-18 playoffs (80 of 82 games; the league baseline covers all 82). Honours cover seven individual awards from 1983-84; one award (1988-89 Sixth Man) is a documented gap.
- **Publication.** The NBA.com, Wikipedia and Wikidata data are used under the owner’s accepted risk for a personal, non-commercial project. No redistribution licence is claimed; the decision to publish remains the owner’s.
