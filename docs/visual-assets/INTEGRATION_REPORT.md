## What was built

### One player-image registry

- **Data.** `frontend/src/features/players/playerImageData.ts` is generated by
  `scripts/assets/build_visual_assets.py` and keyed only by NBA person ID:
  - headshots by the supplying manifest's ID;
  - editorial photos by the IDs already verified in 3.0.
- **Accessor.** `playerImages.ts` reads that data. Each entry has:
  - `kind`: `action` or `headshot`;
  - a responsive `srcSet`, width, height, focal point and alt text;
  - a 192 px face `thumb`;
  - an optional `portrait` alternate for tall frames.
- **Fallback.** Players without an entry keep the photo-free composition, or initials in compact
  lists. Patrick Ewing is the regression case: the supplier excluded his silhouette.

### One season-aware team mark registry

`frontend/src/features/teams/teamLogos.ts` maps each franchise ID to dated eras. `teamLogo(id,
season, name)` returns a mark only when two things hold:

- the season falls inside a verified era;
- the season's own team name matches the era's identity.

If either fails, there is no logo, so a Seattle SuperSonics season can never show a Thunder mark.

### Shared components and styles

- `ui/TeamLogo.tsx`:
  - empty, abbreviation or no fallback;
  - decorative alt text by default;
  - lazy loading.
- `ui/PlayerAvatar.tsx`: face thumbnail or initials, never another face.
- `features/players/PlayerBackdrop.tsx`: a photo behind a page head.
- `design/assets.css`: their styles. No existing stylesheet was rewritten.

### Integrity check in `npm run validate`

`scripts/check-visual-assets.mjs` checks that:

- every registered file exists at its declared size, and no orphan files remain;
- every mark era names the team exactly as the data does, for 604 archive and 653 franchise-record
  team-seasons;
- the social image and icons match the page metadata;
- no source points at a removed image.

## Optimisation

**Formats and variants**

- Every production image is WebP. Photos use quality 86; marks and headshots keep transparency.
- Responsive variants:
  - action photos at 1600, 960 and 640 px (capped at the source width);
  - headshots at 1040, 520 and 360 px;
  - face thumbnails at 192 px;
  - marks at 64 and 256 px.

**Loading**

- `srcset` and `sizes` are written for the real frame, including the extra width a cover crop needs.
- Heroes load with `fetchpriority="high"`; lists, marks and walls load lazily.
- Every `<img>` has intrinsic width and height.

## Known limits

- **2× screens.** At 1440 px and wider, the home and profile heroes have fewer source pixels than
  device pixels (1199 px is the largest supplied source). They are still much sharper than before.
- **Neutral rings.** Seasons whose mark was not supplied show a ring instead of a logo:
  - Seattle 2001-08;
  - Bobcats 2007-14;
  - New Orleans 2002-05 and 2007-08;
  - Nets before 1997;
  - most teams' 1980s–2000s marks.
- **Medium-confidence boundaries.** The eras listed above are the owner's to confirm.
- **Uniform-neutral portraits.** Headshots may show a later team's uniform or post-career attire.
  The site never uses a uniform as data.
